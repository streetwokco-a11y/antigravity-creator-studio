# YouTube Packaging & SEO

## Goal
Optimize YouTube video packaging for maximum CTR and search indexing by generating titles, descriptions, timestamp chapters, tags, and mobile thumbnail legibility briefs.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Formulate 5 high-CTR title variations balancing curiosity, search keywords, and emotional triggers.
2. Draft 120px mobile preview thumbnail legibility brief (verifying high-contrast focal points).
3. Generate detailed video timestamp chapters and search-indexed tags.
4. Output packaging specification artifact.
